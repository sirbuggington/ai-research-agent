Do not enroll unless the school confirms all of this in writing:

Email these questions to each school

Send this before paying an application fee or taking classes. Ask them to reply in writing.

Tip: paste these into an email to nursing admissions or advising.

Essential questions

  1. Is this exact campus and track a program for training new RNs, not RN-to-BSN only?
  2. What exact degree is awarded: AAS, ASN, ADN, BSN, or something else?
  3. Is this exact campus and track on the Tennessee Board nursing school list, and what is its current approval status?
  4. Does graduation qualify me to apply for Tennessee RN licensure by exam and take the NCLEX-RN?
  5. Is the program accredited by a recognized nursing accreditor, such as ACEN, CCNE, or CNEA?
  6. What was the most recent first-time NCLEX-RN pass rate?
  7. What required classes must I finish before applying, and what minimum grades are needed?
  8. Are online required classes, online science labs, or TN eCampus classes accepted?
  9. Is TEAS or HESI required? What score is required, and what score was competitive last cycle?
  10. What are the next deadlines, start dates, number of seats, and admission method?
  11. What is the total estimated cost, including fees and required extras?
  12. Where are labs, practice sessions, and clinicals? Are clinicals days, evenings, weekends, or variable?
  13. Can you send all answers above in writing before enrollment?

Only ask RN-to-BSN questions if the program is Charter Oak or another completion program.
