Stop before enrolling

Do not enroll unless the school confirms all of this in writing:

  • She is not choosing an RN-to-BSN completion program as a starter path.
  • Current Board approval, accreditation, and NCLEX pass rate.
  • Earliest start date and expected RN graduation date from zero credits.
  • Total cost, including fees, supplies, travel, NCLEX, and license costs.
  • Whether Erlanger covers it, and whether there is a service/payback clause.
  • Clinical radius, clinical schedule, and whether sites can be near Chattanooga/Erlanger.
  • Rules about working nights before labs or clinicals.

Call or email these first

Use this before paying an application fee or starting classes. Ask for written answers.

Tip: paste these into an email to nursing admissions or advising.

Critical questions

  1. Erlanger HR: is tuition help reimbursement, direct bill, scholarship, or loan-style assistance?
  2. Erlanger HR: is she eligible as a night-shift PCT, and what is the cap, approved-school list, grade rule, and payback clause?
  3. Erlanger HR: does it cover books, lab fees, uniforms, testing, NCLEX, license fees, and supplies?
  4. School: what is the earliest start date and expected RN graduation date from zero credits?
  5. School: can she apply before all required classes are complete?
  6. School: is TEAS or HESI required, and what score was competitive last cycle?
  7. School: which Sophia/Study.com/CLEP credits are accepted, if any?
  8. School: must A&P, Microbiology, and science labs be taken through an accredited college?
  9. School: what is the current approval/accreditation status and latest NCLEX pass rate?
  10. School: what is total cost, including fees, books, supplies, testing, NCLEX, license, and travel?
  11. School: where are clinicals, and can they be near Chattanooga or Erlanger?
  12. School: are clinicals days, evenings, weekends, or variable?
  13. Both: can she safely keep 3 night shifts, or are there rest/work restrictions before clinical?
  14. Can you send these answers in writing before enrollment?

Do not trust a verbal answer for credits, cost, clinicals, or approval status.
