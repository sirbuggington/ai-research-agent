Tennessee RN path: skip Charter Oak for now.

This guide turns the research note into a simple action plan for a Tennessee resident with high school education, zero college credits, no healthcare credentials, and a goal of becoming an RN in Tennessee as cheaply and legitimately as possible.

Start with the action plan
X

Do not start with Charter Oak.

Charter Oak is an RN-to-BSN completion program. It is not an entry route for someone who is not already an RN.

Best first target

Nearby Tennessee Board-approved public community college AAS/ADN nursing programs.

Cheapest likely route

Community college ADN/AAS plus FAFSA, Tennessee Promise/ReConnect, and Pell if eligible.

Online reality check

Prereqs may be online. Real RN programs still require labs, simulation, and clinicals in person.

Based on research dated June 3, 2026. Always recheck Board approval, costs, admissions windows, and accreditation before enrolling.

The short version

There are only a few decisions that matter at the start. Everything else should be checked against those decisions.

Charter Oak RN/ADN-to-BSN

Not a pre-licensure RN program. It does not create NCLEX-RN eligibility for a non-RN.

Critical checks before enrollment

Program legitimacy
  • Is the exact program pre-licensure RN?
  • Is the exact campus/track on the Tennessee Board list?
  • Does graduation meet the education requirement for Tennessee RN licensure by exam?
  • Is it nursing-accredited by ACEN, CCNE, or CNEA?
Money
  • Ask for total cost, not just tuition.
  • Include books, uniforms, testing, CPR, immunizations, background check, drug screen, NCLEX, licensure, and travel.
  • Ask which costs are not covered by Promise/ReConnect/Pell.
Schedule and clinicals
  • Where are labs, simulation, and clinical rotations?
  • Can clinicals be days, nights, weekends, or variable?
  • Does the school place students, or must students find clinical sites?

Questions to ask admissions

Use these before paying an application fee or taking prerequisites. Ask for answers in writing.

Tip: paste these into an email to nursing admissions or advising.

Legitimacy and licensure

  1. Is this exact program and track pre-licensure RN, or is it RN-to-BSN only?
  2. What exact degree is awarded: AAS, ASN, ADN, BSN, or something else?
  3. Does completing this program meet the educational requirement to apply for Tennessee RN licensure by examination?
  4. Is this exact campus and track listed on the Tennessee Board of Nursing Professional Nursing School List?
  5. What is the current Tennessee Board approval status?
  6. Is the program accredited by ACEN, CCNE, or CNEA, and when is the next review?
  7. What was the most recent first-time NCLEX-RN pass rate?
  8. What is the completion or attrition rate for the most recent cohort?

Admissions and prerequisites

  1. What prerequisites are required before applying?
  2. Which prerequisites can be taken online, and are online science labs accepted?
  3. Is TN eCampus accepted for any prerequisites?
  4. What minimum grade is required for each prerequisite?
  5. Do science courses expire after a certain number of years?
  6. What TEAS/HESI score is required, and what score was competitive for the last accepted cohort?
  7. How many seats are available per cohort?
  8. Is admission points-based, waitlist-based, lottery-based, or first-qualified/first-admitted?
  9. What are the exact application deadlines and next available start dates?

Cost, clinicals, and online claims

  1. What is the total cost including tuition, fees, textbooks, uniforms, testing, background check, drug screen, CPR, immunizations, clinical tracker, NCLEX, and licensure?
  2. Which costs are not covered by Tennessee Promise, ReConnect, or Pell?
  3. Where are labs, simulations, and clinical rotations held?
  4. Are clinical placements guaranteed by the school, or must students find their own?
  5. How far can clinical sites be from the student's home?
  6. Are clinicals days, evenings, weekends, or variable?
  7. Can a student realistically work during the program?
  8. For online/hybrid programs: what parts are asynchronous, live online, and in person?
  9. For RN-to-BSN programs: do you require an active RN license before enrollment or before continued enrollment?
  10. Can you send all answers above in writing before enrollment?
